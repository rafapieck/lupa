# PERSONAS — Lupa

**Version 1** · Week 12 · Both personas are **invented** (not real people). They are built from Rafael's User-role research across Weeks 3–11 and from the scam patterns in official Mexican alerts (Policía Cibernética, Guardia Nacional). Update this file when real users correct them, starting with Week 13's five real users.

---

## Persona 1 — Karla

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / place** | 19 · Ecatepec, Estado de México |
| **Context** | Finished prepa. Lost her call-center job when the company moved to AI agents. Looks for work in Facebook groups and WhatsApp forwards. Android phone with a limited data plan. Not technical; trusts anything that "looks official". Skims instead of reading. |
| **The need, in her words** | *"Nada más quiero saber si es de verdad antes de mandar el dinero del uniforme."* |
| **What she does today** | Asks a friend or her mom, or googles the company name and scrolls. Usually ends up deciding on a hunch. |
| **What she'd pay** | $0. She won't pay to check an offer; she'd pay the scammer $300–$500 for a "uniform" before paying a checker. Lupa is free for her (Charter non-goal). |
| **What would make her quit** | Being asked to sign up or log in; a long form; a result she can't understand in one glance; a warning that reads like "simulated" or "maybe"; anything that uses a lot of data. She leaves silently. |

## Persona 2 — Jorge

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / place** | 27 · Iztapalapa, CDMX |
| **Context** | No formal job for eight months. Takes "lo que salga": driver, warehouse, security guard. Supports his mother. Lives in WhatsApp and in Facebook job groups ("Empleos CDMX urgente"). Android phone with a prepaid plan. Under pressure to say yes fast. |
| **The need, in his words** | *"Me ofrecen chamba en Monterrey con hospedaje y pasaje pagado, pero piden mi INE y salir mañana. ¿Me la juego o no?"* |
| **What he does today** | Asks in the group comments ("¿alguien ya fue?"), and the scammer's own accounts answer "sí, es real". Sometimes asks a cousin. Under time pressure, he goes. |
| **What he'd pay** | $0. Money is too tight. The real cost for him isn't the fee: it's handing over his INE and traveling to an unknown city (the travel-and-housing bait). |
| **What would make him quit** | Anything slow: he has to answer the recruiter today. Results that don't say clearly what to do. Being treated as naive. A page that doesn't work on his data plan. |

---

**Why two different personas:** Karla's risk is losing money up front. Jorge's risk is losing his documents and traveling into an unsafe situation. Both decide alone and blind, facing someone who knows more (the Method's need-gravity). Lupa's core loop must work for both in under 30 seconds.
