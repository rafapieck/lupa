# Real-user sessions — Layer 2 · Week 13

**Rule:** 5 real users, at least 3 strangers to me. Recorded by role, never by name. Their offer text is never copied into this file.

**Script (15–20 min, in person or screen share, on their own phone):**
1. "¿Te ha llegado alguna oferta de trabajo que te dio desconfianza?" (write the peso amount if there was one)
2. Open `www.lupaempleo.xyz` on their phone and check an offer (their own or the example). I don't help, I watch.
3. Ask them to think out loud. Write exact quotes.
4. After the result: "¿Qué harías ahora?" Watch whether they copy the reply or tap the answer card.
5. Close: "¿La usarías la próxima vez? ¿A quién se la mandarías? ¿Te puedo escribir en unos días?"

**Status:** 3 of 5 sessions done · 2 strangers (#1, #3) · 2 more needed, at least 1 stranger.

**What counts:** a behavior (copied, answered, came back, forwarded), not "me gustó".
