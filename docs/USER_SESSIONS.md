**Rule:** 5 real users, at least 3 strangers to me. Recorded by role, never by name. Their offer text is never copied into this file.

**Script (15–20 min, in person or screen share, on their own phone):**
1. "¿Te ha llegado alguna oferta de trabajo que te dio desconfianza?" (write the peso amount if there was one)
2. Open `www.lupaempleo.xyz` on their phone and check an offer (their own or the example). I don't help, I watch.
